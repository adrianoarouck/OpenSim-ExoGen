Exo coordinates

Human .mot/.sto file (IK). The exo is fitted frame by frame and the interaction forces are computed.

–

Each coupling (cuff, belt, foot binding) is a 6-DOF spring-damper between a point on the limb and a point on the exo, coincident at the reference pose. When the exo moves/rotates relative to the limb, a force F = −K·displacement − B·velocity (and a moment) arises, applied at the cuff centre: on the human in one direction and on the exo in the opposite one. The table shows what the exo applies on the human, in the cuff axes: AP (+forward), longitudinal (+towards the proximal joint), ML (+right); M_long is the twist about the limb.
